| R2-05 | C06 spin-down derivation | The rotational-energy loss formula excludes a physical interaction torque after the preceding equation separates it from dipole spin-down. | R2-06 | C12 residual derivation | `y/g` does not remove the additive calibration offset b in `y=gI+b+epsilon`. | Use `(y−b)/g` before foreground subtraction; distinguish the additive calibration offset from physical instrumental intensity so the same term is not subtracted twice. Propagate joint covariance. |
| R2-07 | E08 power variance | A comma appears in `2VI,Cov(V,I)`, obscuring the covariance product. | Write `Var(P)≈I²Var(V)+V²Var(I)+2 V I Cov(V,I)`. |
